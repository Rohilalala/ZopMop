1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website you visit. They are widely used to make websites work, or work more efficiently, and to provide information to site owners.

2. How we use cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies and similar technologies (e.g. localStorage, sessionStorage) to:

  • Keep your session active and secure.
  • Remember your preferences (e.g. dismissed banners).
  • Understand how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • Measure the performance of marketing campaigns when you have consented.

3. Types of cookies we set

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function. These cannot be switched off through our tools.

Performance / Analytics

Help us understand which pages are visited and how the site performs. We aggregate this data; no individual identifiers are stored.

Functional

Remember choices you make to give a more personalised experience.

Marketing

Set only if you opt in to marketing communications. Used to measure ad performance and avoid showing you the same content repeatedly.

4. Third-party cookies

Some pages may include content from third parties (for example, fonts loaded from Google Fonts, form services such as Web3Forms). Those third parties may set their own cookies subject to their own policies. We do not control these cookies.

5. Your choices

You can control cookies via your browser settings — most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. Disabling strictly-necessary cookies may break parts of the site.

6. Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the website. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision.
